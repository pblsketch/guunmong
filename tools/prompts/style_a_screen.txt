A panel from a 19th-century Korean folding screen painting "Guunmongdo" (The Cloud Dream of the Nine), Joseon court painter style. Scene: on a narrow arched stone bridge over a mountain stream on Lotus Peak of Mount Heng, a young Buddhist monk with a shaved head, grey monk robe and wooden prayer beads bows politely, holding a peach-blossom branch, while eight beautiful fairy maidens in flowing Tang dynasty robes with long silk sashes stand across the bridge, smiling shyly. Vivid mineral pigments: malachite green and azurite blue mountains, cinnabar red, gold leaf clouds, white pine trees, misty waterfall. Flat decorative perspective, fine ink outlines, aged silk texture with slight craquelure. Elegant, dreamy, romantic. No text, no letters, no seals, no signature.
