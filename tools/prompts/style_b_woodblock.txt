An illustration in the style of a Korean Joseon-era bangakbon woodblock-printed novel, black ink woodcut lines on warm beige mulberry paper, simple hatching, bold carved outlines, limited flat areas of black, slight ink unevenness of a hand-printed block. Scene: on a narrow arched stone bridge over a mountain stream, a young Buddhist monk with a shaved head, grey monk robe and prayer beads bows politely, holding a blossom branch, while eight fairy maidens in flowing Tang dynasty robes with long silk sashes stand across the bridge, smiling shyly. Pine trees, waterfall and swirling clouds drawn as carved patterns. Monochrome except a very faint light wash. No text, no letters, no seals, no signature.
