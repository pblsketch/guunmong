A single painting split diagonally into two worlds, Korean traditional painting. Left half: monochrome ink-wash painting (sumukhwa) on pale hanji paper, quiet and austere: a young Buddhist monk with a shaved head in a grey robe sits in meditation in a small temple room on Lotus Peak, incense smoke rising, bare pine branches. Right half: the monk's dream bursting into vivid jewel colors in the style of a Joseon folding screen painting: a splendid Tang dynasty palace with gold and cinnabar pavilions, a young scholar-official in a crimson robe and black official hat riding a white horse, eight elegant ladies in colorful silk robes, peach blossoms, gold clouds. The incense smoke from the left half flows into and becomes the gold clouds of the right half. Fine ink lines, mineral pigments, aged paper texture. No text, no letters, no seals, no signature.
